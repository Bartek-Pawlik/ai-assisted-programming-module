# Reading week

No lecture, no lab. This is the week of the Irish October bank holiday.

It sits between teaching week 6 (Agents) and week 7 (MCQ 1), which is
deliberate: it is the week to revise weeks 1–6 rather than the week to
start.

## Worth doing

- Finish any lab you did not complete. Labs 3–6 (prompting, RAG, MCP,
  agents) are all examinable in week 7.
- Try the [practice app](https://danielcregg.is-a.dev/ai-assisted-programming/practice/)
  in exam mode at least once, so the format is not new on the day.
- Read the worked feedback on Practical Assessments 1–5. Each shows it
  once its week has closed, and MCQ 1 draws on the same labs.

## Why this folder is unnumbered

The semester calendar is derived, not stored: reading week is always the
week of the last Monday in October, week 1 begins six weeks before it, and
six teaching weeks sit each side. Numbering this folder would fix in place
something the calendar works out on its own every year.
