# Week 7 — MCQ 1

**Worth 20% of the module.** Held in person during the lab slot.

No lecture and no lab this week.

## What it covers

Weeks 1–6: the module overview, prompting, RAG, MCP, and agents — the
lectures and the labs both. If it was in a deck or in a lab README, it is
examinable.

## How to prepare

- Re-read the lecture decks for weeks 2–6 on the
  [module site](https://danielcregg.is-a.dev/ai-assisted-programming/).
- Work back through the labs you did — the exercises are where most
  questions come from.
- Use the [practice app](https://danielcregg.is-a.dev/ai-assisted-programming/practice/)
  in exam mode: same length and timing as the real thing.

## Format

Multiple choice, sat in the lab under exam conditions. The reading week
immediately before this one is deliberate — use it.
